# Code of Conduct

[Português (Brasil)](pt-BR/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

Oppy welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Keep project discussions respectful, accessible and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence and decisions rather than attacking people.
- Give constructive feedback and accept respectful disagreement.
- Respect other contributors' time, privacy and language preferences.
- Be honest about authorship, AI assistance, validation and limitations.
- Credit prior work and respect its license.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, impersonation, publication of private information without permission and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply to participation in this repository, including issues, pull requests and reviews. They do not restrict independent use or modification under the MIT license.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer if a private channel is available. Otherwise, a public issue may request a private contact method without disclosing sensitive details or identifying affected people. GitHub's reporting tools are also available for platform violations.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or report violations to GitHub. No response time or confidentiality guarantee is promised.
